PCAPiOS

Inspektor HTTP/HTTPS a TCP požadavků, MITM proxy a export PCAP pro iOS (ve stylu PCAPdroid)

Network request inspector and packet capture utility for iOS 5-10 (like PCAPdroid): shows all device HTTP/HTTPS GET & POST requests, links, query arguments, form & JSON values, headers, and TLS SNI to help restore broken legacy apps and inspect unofficial clients. Includes a Substrate hook, local HTTP/CONNECT proxy, request replay over modern TLS, host rewriter, and PCAP/HAR/cURL export.
