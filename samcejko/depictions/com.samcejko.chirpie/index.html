W.I.P. Chirpie

X (Twitter): timeline, threads, profiles, search, posting and DMs

An unofficial X (Twitter) client for iPhone and iPad in the glossy look of iOS 6. Read your For You and Following timelines, open threads and profiles, search tweets and people, see the trends, and post, reply, quote, like, retweet, bookmark, follow and send direct messages - all from your own account. X's site only speaks today's TLS and signs every request with code iOS 6 cannot run, so a tiny helper does that signing; the app carries its own TLS stack and streams photos and video straight from X's own servers. Nothing is stored on the helper: you log in with cookies from a browser (the safer way) or with your username and password.

Features

  • Work-in-progress X client with For You and Following timelines.
  • Open threads and replies, profiles, followers and following lists.
  • Search posts and people and browse trends.
  • Bookmarks and notifications, including a mentions view.
  • Compose posts, replies and quotes with a photo, and delete your own posts.
  • Account actions for likes, reposts, bookmarks, following and unfollowing.
  • Direct-message inbox, conversations and sending messages.
  • Photos, video and tappable web links in posts.
  • Separate device accounts can share one helper service; login cookies remain on the device.
  • Czech and English interface; helper-server and account limitations are listed below.

Good to know

  • Needs a helper server: in Settings set the server address (for samcejko's public one, https://chirpie.samcejko.eu), then log in
  • Log in with cookies from a desktop browser on x.com (auth_token and ct0) - the safer way - or with your username and password. The password is sent once to the helper to fetch the login cookies and is never stored or logged, on the device or the server; only the cookies are kept, on the device. Cookies are the safer option
  • This uses X's web API the unofficial way; heavy automated use can get an account rate-limited or locked. Use your own account and expect occasional limits
