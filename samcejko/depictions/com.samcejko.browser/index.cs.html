Surfari

Webový prohlížeč, který otevře dnešní weby na iOS 6

A modern-web browser for jailbroken iOS 6 Surfari opens today's websites on iOS 6 devices. It brings its own TLS 1.2 connections with current root certificates, rewrites modern CSS and JavaScript for the iOS 6 engine, and adds ad blocking, a reader view, saved passwords and private browsing. Made for iOS 6.x on armv7 devices (tested on an iPad 2 with iOS 6.1.2). The page engine is iOS 6's own WebKit, which gets no security updates: read the security notice in the README.
