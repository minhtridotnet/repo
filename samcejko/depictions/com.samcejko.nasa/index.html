NASA

The official NASA app of 2013, back online: missions, images, videos, news, NASA TV and Third Rock Radio

NASA's own iOS app, version 3.12 from 2013, exactly as NASA shipped it, with one change: it talks to a small server of mine instead of NASA's retired mobile backend. The server answers the app's old API with today's data (missions and pictures from NASA's live service, news and features from nasa.gov, videos from NASA's YouTube channels, the NASA TV live stream and Third Rock Radio) over plain http, so a 2012 device can read it. Nothing else in the app was touched.

Features

  • Mission pages with descriptions, banners, launch dates and countdowns.
  • ISS tracking on a map.
  • Astronomy Picture of the Day and NASA Image of the Day with full descriptions.
  • Favorites and bookmarks for image browsing.
  • NASA-center video feeds played through the system video player.
  • Read nasa.gov news and features by topic inside the app.
  • NASA TV and Third Rock Radio streams provided through the configured proxy.
  • NASA-center maps, solar-system pages and mission details.
  • Use available cached data if the replacement service cannot be reached; the original app interface is English.

Good to know

  • Tweets are empty: Twitter no longer offers a free API. ISS sighting predictions and the Launch Services Program feeds are gone too
  • Everything goes through nasa.samcejko.eu; when that server is down the app shows its cached data
  • NASA's app, not mine; English only. Not affiliated with NASA
