Backcap

Limits background apps (0–6) and closes unused apps when locking the screen

A SpringBoard tweak for iOS 6 that keeps background applications under control so you do not have to double-click the physical Home button and close apps by hand. Set how many background apps may stay open (0 to close every app as soon as you leave it with the Home button, 1 to 6 apps, or Unlimited), choose whether to close the oldest app in the switcher or the previous background app, and let Backcap close all unused background apps automatically when you lock the screen. Apps playing music or audio in the background (such as Pandora, Music, Sixify or Muzika), recording audio or in a phone call are kept open automatically.

Features

  • Set a limit of 0–6 apps kept in the background.
  • A limit of zero closes an app as soon as you leave it with Home.
  • Choose whether an exceeded limit closes the oldest switcher app or the last-used background app.
  • Closed apps are removed from the iOS 6 multitasking bar automatically.
  • Close unused background apps when locking the device or turning off its screen.
  • Optionally keep the active foreground app when locking.
  • Automatically protect apps playing background audio, recording audio or handling a phone call.
  • Maintain a personal Protected Apps list.
  • Close background apps immediately from Settings → Backcap; settings apply without a respring.
  • Czech and English settings.

Good to know

  • Configure in Settings → Backcap; changes take effect immediately without a respring
  • Tested on iOS 6.1.3 (armv7: iPhone 4S, iPad 2 and other iOS 6.x devices)
