CloudWave

SoundCloud music streaming, search, waveform player and playlists for iOS 5–10

CloudWave is a native SoundCloud streaming client for iOS 5–10 featuring the classic iOS 6 Aqua look. Search millions of tracks, explore trending music by genre, stream audio in the background through an on-device TLS proxy, scrub through interactive audio waveforms, and sign in or sync your SoundCloud profile likes and playlists.

Features

  • Direct connection to SoundCloud API v2 with automatic dynamic client_id discovery.
  • Stream progressive MP3 and HLS audio tracks on iOS 5–10 via local TLS 1.3 localhost relay.
  • Interactive SoundCloud waveform visualizer with touch scrubbing and glossy album art.
  • Full-text search across tracks and artists plus genre filtering (Electronic, Hip-Hop, Rock, Ambient, Pop, Classical).
  • Background audio playback with lock-screen metadata and remote control support.
  • Save liked tracks and playlists locally or sync with your SoundCloud username/OAuth token.
  • Custom CoreGraphics vector icons in tab bar and Now Playing transport controls (zero emoji).
  • Classic iOS 6 Aqua interface in Czech and English.

Good to know

  • Tested on iPhone 4S (iOS 6.1.3) and compatible with iOS 5.0–10.3.4.
  • Unofficial client, not affiliated with SoundCloud.
