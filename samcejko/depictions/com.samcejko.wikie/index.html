Wikie

Offline Wikipedia and OpenZIM reader for iOS 5–10

Read Wikipedia, Wiktionary, Project Gutenberg, and educational archives completely offline on your vintage iPhone and iPad in the classic glossy look of iOS 6. Wikie opens modern and legacy .zim archives with fast binary search over millions of articles, full decompression (Zstandard and LZMA2/zlib), embedded images and stylesheets, instant search suggestions, and reading history and bookmarks. Transfer ZIM books easily over Wi-Fi right from your desktop browser or via iTunes File Sharing.

Features

  • Read downloaded ZIM archives without an internet connection.
  • Use compatible Wikipedia, Wiktionary, Gutenberg and StackExchange archives.
  • Indexed article lookup and fast search suggestions across large archives.
  • Support for modern Zstandard and older Deflate/LZMA2-compressed archives.
  • Turn on Wi-Fi Upload and send ZIM files directly from a computer's browser.
  • Alternatively transfer books over USB through iTunes File Sharing.
  • Reading history and bookmarked articles.
  • A styled article reader with adjustable text size.
  • Internal archive links open within Wikie.
  • Memory-conscious archive caching for older devices.

Good to know

  • To add books: turn on Wi-Fi Upload in the app library and open the displayed IP address in your computer's browser, or drag .zim files into Wikie in iTunes File Sharing
  • ZIM files can be downloaded from download.kiwix.org (choose 'nopic' or 'mini' versions for older 16GB/32GB devices for best performance)
  • Native ARMv7 optimization with memory-efficient LRU cache to prevent low-memory termination on 512MB RAM devices

What's new in 1.0.8

  • Restored the opaque classic blue navigation bar on iPhone.
