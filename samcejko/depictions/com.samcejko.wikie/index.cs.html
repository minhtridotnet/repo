Wikie

Offline čtečka Wikipedie a OpenZIM pro iOS 5–10

Offline Wikipedia and ZIM reader for iOS 5-10 Wikie brings the world's knowledge to iOS 5-10 devices without an internet connection. Supports both modern and legacy .zim archives (v5/v6) with Zstandard and Deflate decompression, instant title index search, offline UIWebView rendering, iTunes File Sharing, built-in Wi-Fi upload and an online catalog downloader.
